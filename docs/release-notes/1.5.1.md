1.5.1 is released! Fixes for mods whose configs use custom JSON converters, found through C&N Jewelry.

For players

* With "AutoConfigs": true, a world with C&N Jewelry installed crashed while loading, with nothing in the log. It now loads, and C&N Jewelry gets a settings screen.
* Settings that a mod saves in a compact form, such as a list written as "a,b,c", were wiped when ConfigKit read them. In C&N Jewelry that emptied the socket rules and broke the world on load. They now reach the mod intact, and show on the settings screen as raw JSON.
* A value ConfigKit cannot hand to the mod no longer replaces the mod's own value with nothing. The mod keeps what it had, and the verbose log names the setting.

For mod authors

* A member with [JsonConverter] or [JsonProperty(ItemConverterType = ...)] is read, validated and written through that converter. Lists, dictionaries and objects show as raw JSON; an enum under StringEnumConverter keeps its dropdown.
* A JsonObject member shows as raw JSON. A JsonItemStack's resolved stack, or any ItemStack, Block or Item member, is left out of the settings screen, and ConfigKit never assigns it.
